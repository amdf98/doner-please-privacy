4. Consent and your choices

On first launch the game shows Google's consent form (IAB TCF v2.2). Without your consent, personalized ads, ad measurement and analytics stay off; the game still works and may show non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Policy (privacy options), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland

Our partners also process data on servers outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland, mainly in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the partner is certified) and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Cloud save and player account: until you delete them in Settings or ask us to (delete your account).

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15-21 GDPR), and you can withdraw consent at any time for the future (Art. 7(3)). Email us; we reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in your German federal state, the Austrian Data Protection Authority or the Swiss FDPIC.

8. Permissions

The game only requests permissions needed to run. It does not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

9. Children

Döner Please is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Under 16, you need a parent's agreement for personalized ads and analytics; otherwise decline in the consent form. Parents can ask us by email to delete data.

10. In-game purchases

Google Play shows all prices in your currency including VAT. Purchases are digital content delivered right away; Google Play's terms apply to your 14-day right of withdrawal. Subscriptions (Döner Club) renew until you cancel them in Google Play.

11. Security and changes

We rely on our partners' platform security. If we change this policy we post the new version here with a new date and tell you in the game about important changes.

12. Contact

Questions and privacy requests to UBTOP Studio: UBTOPstudio@gmail.com
